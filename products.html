Our Breads, Pastries, and Cakes

Our selection changes with the season and the day's bake. The ranges below are a guide; contact us for current availability and preorder details.

Breads

Slow-fermented loaves are baked for a crisp crust and flavorful crumb. Daily choices may include country sourdough, seeded wheat, and soft sandwich bread.

Typical price range: $6-$12 per loaf.

Pastries and sweets

Choose from rotating morning pastries, cookies, bars, muffins, and other small-batch sweets. Seasonal flavors appear throughout the year.

Typical price range: $3-$6 each; assorted boxes from $18.

Cakes and celebration desserts

Celebration cakes are available by preorder in classic flavors with simple seasonal decoration. Dessert trays are also available for meetings and family events.

Typical price range: $32-$85, depending on size and customization.

For special requests, use our inquiry and preorder form.
