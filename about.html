About North Star Bakery

Our story

North Star Bakery began with a simple goal: make dependable, handmade baked goods and create a welcoming neighborhood stop. What started with a few bread recipes grew into a daily selection of loaves, pastries, cookies, and celebration desserts for regular customers and special occasions.

We still work in small batches so our bakers can pay attention to fermentation, texture, and the details that make each bake worth sharing.

Our sourcing philosophy

We choose ingredients for quality, consistency, and seasonality. Whenever practical, we work with regional suppliers for flour, dairy, fruit, honey, and other staples. Menus shift during the year so we can feature ingredients that are at their best.

Customers with food allergies should tell us about their needs before ordering. Our kitchen handles common allergens, so we cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.
